A maintained fork of **SDR++**, the cross-platform open-source
software-defined-radio application. The work is focused on targeted support for
**RFNM** hardware.

### Main activities

- Maintaining RFNM-specific changes.
- Integrating those changes with the upstream C++ codebase.
- Preserving the original documentation and attribution.
- Remaining aligned with SDR++'s **GPL-3.0** licence.

### My contribution

Maintenance of the fork and its focused hardware changes. This description
clearly separates that contribution from the upstream SDR++ project and avoids
claiming authorship of the full application.
