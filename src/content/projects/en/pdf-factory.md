A pipeline that generates product datasheets as **PDFs in 6 languages** from an
Excel file, and publishes them online automatically.

Built in **Node.js** with an HTML/CSS → PDF approach, image optimisation, secure
credential handling and regression tests. Generation **runs automatically via
cron** on a dedicated, hardened virtual machine, with no manual step.

It modernised a previous legacy component, making the process fully automatic
and repeatable.

## My contribution

Analysing the pre-existing system, designing the modular architecture and the
multilingual system, choosing the stack and the security measures.
