This website is designed with your privacy in mind.

## No tracking
The site is **static** and **does not use profiling cookies**, nor analytics
tools that identify you. There are no advertising pixels or social widgets that
track your browsing. Fonts are self-hosted or system fonts: no requests are made
to external services that could collect your IP address.

## Contact form
If you use the contact form, the data you enter (name, email, message) is used
**only to reply to your request**. It is never shared with third parties nor used
for marketing. Delivery happens through a transactional email provider; messages
are kept only for as long as needed to handle the conversation.

## Your rights (GDPR)
Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 you have the right to access, rectify, erase and
object to the processing of your data. To exercise them, write to the contact
email listed on the site.
